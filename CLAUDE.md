# HVNF Studios — Studio Operating System

## Voice
Talk chill and casual, friendly terms. Do all the work, but explain what things do along the way (what a library is for, what a command did, why a pattern exists). Educational, not a lecture.

High-performance web dev + digital modernization studio. We replace broken legacy sites for local / regional / high-ticket service businesses (automotive, trades, medical, fitness, dining, pro services) with fast bespoke web apps that capture calls, bookings, and trust.
- Philosophy: **Show, don't tell.** Build working mobile-first previews from a business's public footprint, hand them to the decision-maker, remove purchase friction.
- Team: Lead Architect/Product (frontend, Next.js, motion, deploys) + Nathan (outreach, lead gen, closing). 50/50 net profit.

## Anti-"vibe-coding" standards
**Visual bans:** purple-on-black neon, electric gradients, pastel blobs, mock terminals, dot grids, blurry radial orbs, glassmorphism, `Sparkles` icons, generic 3-feature bento grids, identical 3-tier pricing cards, colored left-border stripe cards, bouncing arrows, infinite spinners, aggressive hover zooms.
**Motion (client work):** subtle, performant reveals only.

### Showcase pieces are the exception — go all out
The bans above exist to stop *client* sites (plumbers, clinics, detailers) looking like AI slop. Portfolio showcase pieces have the opposite job: prove range. On a showcase build, **go maximal**:
- WebGL/3D, GPU particle fields, transforming and exploding models, scroll-scrubbed GSAP timelines, pinned sequences, kinetic typography, magnetic and parallax interactions, shader effects.
- Target feel: a motion-designed film — After Effects, not a brochure. Visual dopamine is the point.
- Use the whole toolkit, not a subset: `lenis` (smooth scroll), `gsap` + `ScrollTrigger`, `three` + `@react-three/fiber` + `@react-three/drei`, `motion/react` (Framer Motion, renamed), `border-beam`, `liquid-gooey`, `sonner`, `canvas-confetti`, plus the recipes in `reference/transitions-dev-snippets.md`.
- Still banned even here: the *cheap* tells — purple/indigo SaaS neon, fake terminals, dot grids, `Sparkles` icons, glassmorphism. Aggressive is fine; generic is not.

**Non-negotiable on every build, showcase included:** a visitor must know **what the product is within three seconds**. Lead with plain language, then earn the jargon. Technical vocabulary is seasoning, not the meal — if the hero doesn't say what the thing *is*, the build has failed no matter how good the motion is.
**Palettes by industry:** Trades/Auto → slate `#09090b`, steel, amber/burnt orange. Medical/Dental → neutral whites, clinic slate, surgical blue, sage. Luxury → bone, charcoal, muted brass, serif pairings.
**Copy bans:** "It's not X, it's Y"; tech jargon for non-tech brands ("streamlined", "supercharged", "next-gen", "paradigm"). Speak to real pain points ("Same-Day Emergency Service", "Upfront Estimates").
**Social proof:** never fabricate testimonials; pull real review sentiment. Always ship working Terms + Privacy links.
